## 🔍 Project Highlights

### Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Intelligence App

A deployed machine learning app that predicts customer churn and segments customers by risk level.

**Key Features**

* CSV upload
* Data validation
* Churn probability prediction
* Customer risk segmentation
* Monitoring dashboard
* Downloadable prediction results

**Why it matters:**
Businesses can use this type of system to identify at-risk customers earlier and make better retention decisions.

---

### Vendor Invoice Intelligence Portal

A finance-focused machine learning app for invoice approval risk and freight cost prediction.

**Key Features**

* Freight cost prediction
* Invoice risk screening
* Structured input workflow
* Business insight cards
* Interactive Streamlit dashboard

**Why it matters:**
This type of tool can reduce manual invoice checking and help teams make faster approval decisions.

---

### AirSense AI

A full-stack AQI monitoring and prediction platform built for environmental intelligence.

**Key Features**

* Live AQI data workflow
* Pollutant trend visualization
* AQI prediction
* Health-risk alerts
* Map-based air quality view
* Rule-based AQI assistant

**Why it matters:**
It turns raw air quality readings into understandable insights for safer outdoor decisions.
