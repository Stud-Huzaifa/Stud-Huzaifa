01 / About
I'm Muhammad Huzaifa, a BS Data Science student at Dawood University of Engineering & Technology in Karachi, Pakistan. I enjoy taking a real-world problem through the full data workflow: preparing the data, exploring patterns, building and evaluating a model, and turning the result into an application people can use.
- Interested in: Machine Learning, predictive analytics, data products, and practical AI systems
- Working with: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, SQL, Flask, Streamlit, and FastAPI
- Building: Churn prediction, invoice intelligence, environmental monitoring, and forecasting projects
- Open to: Data science internships, junior data roles, ML projects, and remote collaboration
A model is only useful when its output helps someone make a better decision.
